Camera Calibration

One-off per device. Place a printed QR sticker flat on a wall or the steel. Measure the printed black square, then measure the distance from the camera lens to the sticker surface with a tape (about 500-1000 mm works well). Hold the device square-on to the sticker so the readout says "good", then tap Save calibration.
